What we handle

The unglamorous half of the job.

Most creators do not stall because the content got worse. They stall because one person cannot film, edit, post, pitch, invoice and negotiate at the same time. That is the half we take.

Brand deals, end to end

We source the partners, pitch you properly, and negotiate the rate. You see the contract with our notes on it before anything gets signed. We do not take deals you would be embarrassed to post.

Editing and post

Long form cutdowns, vertical shorts, thumbnails, captions, packaging. You send the footage, we send back something ready to publish on a schedule you can actually keep.

Growth strategy

What to post, where, and why. We read your analytics with you every month and change one thing at a time, so you can tell what is working instead of guessing.

Money, plural

Sponsorships are one income stream and a fragile one. We build the others with you: your own storefront, memberships, affiliate, licensing, live. The goal is that no single platform can end your year.

Contracts and admin

Invoicing, chasing late payers, usage rights, exclusivity windows, tax paperwork. The boring things that quietly cost creators thousands a year when nobody is watching them.

People who answer

A small roster on purpose. You get a real person on a real call, not a ticket number and a quarterly check in from someone who has not watched your content.

Our roster is private.

That is policy, not modesty. Brands get the full list, the real numbers and the audience data under NDA. Everyone else gets the total at the top of this page and nothing more.

  • Nothing changes on your feedNo agency tag in your bio, no announcement post, no credit line. You are never listed anywhere public, including here.

Who we sign

There is no follower minimum.

We have signed creators under 10k and creators over a million. What we are reading for is whether the thing you are building has somewhere to go.

  • You post consistentlyNot daily. Just predictably enough that we can build a plan on top of it.
  • Your audience is genuinely yoursReal engagement on a smaller account beats a bought number every time, and brands can tell.
  • You want this to be the jobWe are not the right fit for a hobby account, and that is a completely fine thing to have.
  • You are easy to work withReplies to messages, hits agreed dates, tells us early when something slips. That is the whole bar.
